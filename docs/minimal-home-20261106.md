# Homepage refinement · 6 November 2026

## Design

Reference: https://www.apple.com.cn/ (inspected 6 November 2026). Adopted generous spacing, large project imagery, short headings and clear blue actions. Soft green project surfaces, blue demos, a violet research accent and a warm learning background add section identity without changing action colors. The system font hierarchy uses 16–17px body text, 14px navigation and at least 13px for the redesigned secondary labels. Mobile inputs remain 16px to avoid focus zoom. Project screenshots, content and identity belong to this portfolio. No Apple media, fonts or marks are redistributed.

The introduction is factual rather than promotional. The manual three-project preview uses a larger image and quieter controls. Additional project implementation details use native disclosures. Research claims and publication status are retained. The timeline keeps its dated notes and a separately labeled practice plan.

The homepage replaces its previous thin top line with a current-section label, progress track and percentage. The percentage measures scroll position in the currently visible document; it is not a record of reading or study completion. Content changes trigger recalculation. The strip is hidden without JavaScript and in print.
