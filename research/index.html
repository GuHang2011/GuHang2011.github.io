KICP · Multimodal Fake-News Detection

Frozen representations, adaptation and fusion.

Question and implementation

02 / LEARNING & REPRODUCIBILITY

KAPT Reproducibility Lab

KANO-derived local study · Explicit source attribution.

Question and changes

What makes evaluation helpers trustworthy? This focused study organizes early-stopping state, prediction alignment and synthetic checks from a local KANO/KAPT project. The third-party KANO baseline and local engineering changes are identified separately.

Case & validation replay ↗Code & reproduction ↗

Evidence to inspect

  • Best epoch versus stopped epoch, strict improvement and finite-score checks.
  • Matching sample IDs and labels before averaging predictions.
  • No trained molecular benchmark is claimed; the public examples are synthetic learning tools.
