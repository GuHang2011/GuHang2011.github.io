01 / The question I want to pursue

When labels and compute are limited, can multimodal improvements survive changes in validation, modality quality and decision thresholds? For proposed doctoral work, I want to investigate this question in reliable emotion recognition.

The current fake-news study motivates the evaluation question. Moving to emotion recognition requires new datasets, tasks and controlled experiments; it is not an already validated transfer result.

Read the existing evidence first, then the proposed first study. The climate project is an interdisciplinary application; the proposed doctoral question remains reliable multimodal evaluation.

05 / Proposed doctoral work

  1. Budget-aware adaptation

    Count training, model-selection and calibration labels together; compare under matched budgets.

  2. Evidence-aware decisions

    Separate modality quality, disagreement and calibrated confidence; examine selective prediction.

  3. Continual adaptation

    Fix replay and update budgets, and evaluate both new-task performance and forgetting.

These are proposed studies awaiting experiments. Engineering experience supports data checks, repeatable experiment interfaces and traceable versions; controlled research must establish the method claims.
