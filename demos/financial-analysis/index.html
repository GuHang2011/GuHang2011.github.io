Methods & boundaries

Five financial ratios

Gross margin, receivables/revenue, operating cash/revenue, liabilities/assets and expenses/revenue. Missing inputs and zero denominators remain explicit; observed zero values are retained.

Exploratory preprocessing

Annual medians → StandardScaler → KMeans (k = 2/3/4, n_init = 20, seed = 42). PCA projects the same five standardized features to two dimensions; it is a display, not the clustering input.

No predictive validation

All records are synthetic. There are no ground-truth risk labels, held-out predictive tests or a validated best k. Cluster numbers are arbitrary, fitted independently by year, and have no ranking meaning.
