Where do fusion gains come from in low-resource multimodal fake news detection? Operating points, archive artifacts and frozen CLIP baselines

Reanalyzes 344 archived runs to separate fusion effects, operating-point changes and archive artifacts. FCP is the fusion model under study, not a newly proposed architecture.

Study overview ↗216 records recomputed ↗Analysis code & saved results ↗Proposed next questions ↗
Three takeaways and the evaluation boundary
01

Compare the operating point

Some low-shot GossipCop Macro-F1 advantages track a change in the decision operating point; a higher score alone does not establish a stronger representation.

02

Audit the data

Archive markers and near-duplicate content can confound evaluation on recollected news datasets.

03

Account for the full budget

The manuscript limits training labels but uses a fully labeled validation set. The proposed work will count selection and calibration labels too.

Scope: two English recollected news datasets. The public package supports post-hoc metrics from saved predictions; original training and split reconstruction need additional inputs. Hard labels cannot recover AUROC or score calibration.

2 Nov check: 216 result rows matched the published reference; this is an artifact check, not new training.
